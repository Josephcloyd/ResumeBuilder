About & FAQ

Free resume builder for Filipino job seekers, ready in minutes.

ResumeFlow is a free online resume builder and CV maker by GetLaunch PH. You build your resume in the browser with a live preview, then download it as a PDF in A4, Letter, Short Bond, Long Bond or Legal size. There is no sign-up and no watermark, and your resume stays on your own device.

Start your resume, it's free

What you can do with ResumeFlow

  • Live preview while you type

    Edit on one side and watch the resume update on the other. What you see is exactly what your PDF will look like.

  • 8 professional templates

    Switch templates anytime without losing what you typed.

  • PDF in Philippine paper sizes

    Short Bond and Long Bond, plus A4, Letter and Legal.

  • Completeness score

    The Review tab scores your resume and lists what is missing.

  • Works on your phone

    Edit, preview and download from a mobile browser. Changes save automatically.

  • Design controls

    12 fonts, accent colors, margins, spacing, one or two columns, and a profile photo.

How to make a resume with ResumeFlow

  1. Open ResumeFlow

    Go to resumeflow.getlaunchph.com. Start from a sample resume or click New Resume.

  2. Fill in your details

    Add your contact information, summary, work experience, education, skills and any other sections you need. Reorder or hide sections freely.

  3. Choose a template and style

    Pick one of 8 templates, then adjust fonts, colors, spacing and paper size in the Design tab.

  4. Check your score

    Open the Review tab to see your completeness score and suggestions.

  5. Download your PDF

    Click Download PDF. Your resume is ready to print or send.

Your resume stays private

ResumeFlow saves your resumes only in your browser on your device. Your resume content is not uploaded to our servers. On a shared computer, export your data and use Delete my local data on the My Resumes page when you are done.

The only information sent to us is what you choose to include in a bug report.

Frequently asked questions

Is ResumeFlow free?

Yes. ResumeFlow is completely free. There is no paid plan, no sign-up and no watermark on your PDF.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the site and start building right away. There is no account, email or password.

Where is my resume saved?

Your resumes are saved only in your own browser on your device. They are not uploaded to a server. To move a resume to another device, export it as a JSON file and import it there.

Can I make a resume on my phone?

Yes. ResumeFlow works in mobile browsers. You can switch between the editor and the live preview and download the PDF from your phone.

What paper sizes can I download?

A4 (210 × 297 mm), Letter (8.5 × 11 in), Short Bond (8.5 × 11 in), Long Bond (8.5 × 13 in) and Legal (8.5 × 14 in).

Is there an ATS-friendly resume template?

Yes. The Simple ATS template uses plain, parser-friendly formatting with no graphics, which suits online application systems.

Can I add a photo to my resume?

Yes. You can upload and crop a profile photo, choose a circle, rounded or square shape, adjust its size, or hide it.

How do I report a bug?

Tap the Report a bug button at the bottom right of ResumeFlow, describe what happened and optionally attach a screenshot.
